Home / About

Three generations of doing it right.

Revival Contracting & Remodeling is a third-generation residential contractor built on experience, integrity, honesty and quality workmanship. We were founded on Christian principles.

Owner Dan Ekberg has owned and operated his own contracting companies since 1986. He is known in the Central Florida construction industry for his knowledge of the trade and the integrity he brings to every job site.

We run as a multi-disciplined, fast-paced residential contractor that treats quality and timeliness as essential. Dan helps clients with design ideas and selections. He supervises the work and ensures that everything has been done professionally and properly.
